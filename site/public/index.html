Mattermost, at native speed.

Matty is a fast, native Mattermost client for macOS, Linux, Windows and the web. One small process, no browser inside. It opens in a fraction of a second and keeps working offline.

Download Matty Try the demo in your browser

Free and open source. For macOS, Linux and Windows, or open it in your browser.

Light on your machine.

Everything you do in Mattermost, quicker.

Peek before you switch

Rest on an unread channel to read what is new, then mark it read or open it. Pills point at unreads above and below, and a hidden sidebar slides in from the edge.

Threads beside the channel

Reply and follow without losing your place. Collapsed reply threads, following, and every thread you are in, in one view with an Unread filter.

Jump anywhere

Press Ctrl K, or ⌘ K on a Mac, to find any channel or person across teams and servers, unreads first. Mattermost’s keyboard shortcuts work too.

Make it yours

Seven built-in themes, each with a light and a dark mode, or build your own from an accent color, a tint and a corner shape. Matty works every color out from contrast rules, so text stays readable. Bring your own fonts, and share a theme as a snippet.

  • Offline first

    Channels you have opened read from a local cache. Messages you send offline wait in an outbox and go out in order.

  • Every way to sign in

    Password, MFA or single sign-on with GitLab, Google, Office 365, OpenID or SAML. Several teams and servers at once.

  • Messages, fully

    Markdown with highlighted code and tables, reactions, files and images, edits, pins, saved and scheduled messages.

  • Find anything

    Search, recent mentions, saved and pinned messages, channel files and drafts.

  • Keyboard and screen readers

    One tab stop per list and an accessible name on every control.

  • Updates itself

    On macOS, Matty installs signed updates on its own. A new beta ships about once a week.
